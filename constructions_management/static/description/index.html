Complete Feature Set

Everything a construction contractor needs, in one app

Twelve integrated modules tracing every quantity and financial transaction through Company → Project → WBS → BOQ Line → Cost Code → Analytic Account.

BOQ & Budget Control BOQ templates are not locked to one house size — save and reuse as many templates as you need (3.5 Marla, 10 Marla, 1 Kanal, commercial, custom) and load any of them onto a project in one click. Each line supports either a manual quantity or a dimensional formula (Gross Qty = Number × Length × Width × Height, Net Qty = Gross − Deduction, Procurement Qty = Net × (1 + Waste %)), plus Excel import/export in the same column layout estimators already use. Once a BOQ revision is approved it becomes the immutable baseline — write-protected at the database level — and any further change must go through a new revision or a governed Variation, never a silent edit. Every line also tracks budget control in real time: Original, Revised, Committed (open PO value), Actual Cost, Estimate-to-Complete, Estimate-at-Completion, and Variance/Variance % against the baseline, plus separate quantity, rate and usage variance breakdowns. Rate Analysis Build a defensible unit rate for any BOQ item from its actual cost components — material, labour, equipment, subcontract, transport, overhead and margin lines each contribute their own quantity and rate. Rate analyses are effective-dated, so you can keep a history as market rates move, and the resulting rate can be applied directly onto a BOQ line with one action, keeping the audit trail of exactly how that price was built. Variations & Change Control Every change request moves through a fixed governance chain — Technical Review, Commercial Evaluation, Internal Approval, then Customer Approval — before it can be marked Approved. Each variation line records the original and revised quantity/rate, the resulting cost effect and revenue effect (with a separate customer-facing rate), and a schedule effect in days. Only lines on an approved variation are allowed to change a BOQ line's Revised Qty/Rate/Amount — rejected or pending variations never touch the baseline. Procurement Material Requests are governed by a boxed allowance formula — Available to Request = Revised BOQ Qty − Already-Approved Requests − Already-Ordered Qty — so a site engineer physically cannot request more material against a line than the contract allows. Approved requests convert in one click into Purchase Requisitions (for competitive sourcing) or internal transfers, and every purchase order line traces back to the BOQ line and cost code that funded it, all the way through to the vendor bill. Site Inventory Confirming a project auto-creates its own set of stock locations — Receiving, Quality Hold, Accepted, Issued, Consumed, Return and Scrap — so every project's material is tracked in complete isolation from every other project. Site receipts capture challan and inspection detail on arrival; consumption posts through a virtual "Consumed" location so Odoo's own stock valuation engine raises the correct Dr WIP / Cr Inventory journal entry automatically; wastage above a configurable threshold requires explicit approval; and inter-project transfers and periodic cycle counts are built in as first-class documents, not workarounds. Subcontracting & RA Billing A subcontract agreement fixes the scope and rate per BOQ line for a subcontractor. Work is measured through joint measurement sheets that must pass Site Engineer → QS → PM → Commercial verification before they're payable. RA (Running Account) Certificates then compute Gross Value = Qty × Rate, Cumulative = Previous + Current, Retention = Gross × Retention %, an advance-recovery deduction capped by the agreement terms, and a final Net Payable after extras, retention, recoveries and penalties — with a one-click action to generate the matching vendor bill. Execution Daily Site Reports capture manpower by trade, equipment hours, subcontractor headcount, executed quantities against BOQ lines, delays, instructions and quality notes for a single day and location. Work Orders group planned material, labour and equipment resource lines against a WBS phase and track planned-vs-actual for each. Per-WBS Cost Sheets then roll all of this — material, labour, equipment, subcontract — into one place for that phase. WIP & Revenue Recognition Month-end WIP periods compute percent-complete on a cost-to-cost basis: eligible cost incurred to date divided by the latest estimated total eligible cost. Each project runs under one of two fixed accounting profiles — Contractor or Property Developer — and closing a period posts real journal entries: Dr Contract Cost / Cr Contract WIP for cost recognition, and Dr Contract Asset (or Progress Billing) / Cr Contract Revenue for revenue recognition, so your P&L reflects percentage-of-completion accounting, not just cash received. Progress Billing Customer Progress Certificates are driven entirely by certified quantities: current work done, cumulative work against the revised contract value, stored (delivered but not yet installed) material, retention withheld and advance recovered — netting down to the amount actually invoiced to the customer. Approving a certificate generates the real customer invoice directly, with no manual re-entry of figures. Earned Value & Forecasting Every WBS phase (and each project, rolled up from its phases) computes the full EVM formula set — Budget at Completion, Planned Value, Earned Value, Actual Cost, Cost Variance, Schedule Variance, Cost Performance Index, Schedule Performance Index, Estimate at Completion, Estimate to Complete and Variance at Completion — with safe zero-division handling. Red/Amber/Green thresholds (defaults: green ≥ 0.95, amber 0.85–0.949, red < 0.85 on CPI/SPI) are configurable in Settings, and drive the RAG counts shown on the dashboard. Quality & HSE Inspection & Test Plans define what must be checked and when; Quality Inspections record the actual result against them and can raise a Non-Conformance Report or a snag-list entry when work doesn't pass. On the safety side, HSE Toolbox Talks, HSE Inspections and Incident records are tracked with a 4×4 (likelihood × severity) risk matrix, giving every site a documented, auditable safety trail rather than paper checklists. Equipment & Document Control Equipment usage logs (owned or rented) post their hours as analytic cost against the project and WBS automatically, so machine time shows up in the same cost sheets and EVM numbers as labour and material. A dedicated document register keeps drawings, permits and contracts under revision control, with every version and approval captured on the chatter as an evidence trail.

How It Works

From tender to handover, one traceable thread

1 Set up the project Pick a category, sub-category and team; auto-generate the WBS phase tree from a reusable stage template. 2 Load or build the BOQ Start from a saved template or build lines manually, submit through negotiation and contract, then approve the immutable baseline. 3 Procure and stock materials Raise material requests against the BOQ allowance, convert to purchase requisitions or transfers, receive at site with challan/inspection details. 4 Execute and report Issue material to work areas, log daily site reports and work orders, record consumption and let physical progress update automatically. 5 Manage subcontractors Agree scope and rates, measure work through a multi-role approval chain, certify and pay through RA certificates. 6 Close the books Run month-end WIP, recognize revenue, bill the customer through progress certificates, and track earned value to completion.
