Capture tender submissions with their purpose, tender number, mode of instrument and supporting documents — approve or reject with one click, and turn an accepted tender straight into a sales Quotation.

📋 Tender Details

Record the Customer, Purpose (Bid Money/Earnest Money, Performance Security, Advance Payment Security) and the Tender Number on every submission.

💳 Mode of Instrument

Choose how the tender is secured: Cash, Pay Order/CDR, Bank Guarantee or Insurance Guarantee.

📎 Typed Attachments

Attach the Tender Document, Bank Guarantee Format or any Other file, each tagged with its own type.

✅ Accept / Reject

A simple one-click approval flow moves a tender from Draft to Accepted or Rejected, with full status tracking.

🔁 Convert to Quotation

Accepted tenders convert into a sale Quotation in one click — customer, reference and attachments carry over automatically.

📜 Full Audit Trail

Every change is logged in the chatter, so you always know who accepted, rejected or converted a tender.
