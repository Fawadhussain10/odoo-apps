The core clinical and administrative engine of PackBytes HMS — patient records, appointments, prescriptions, treatments and procedures, medical alerts and billing, all tied to one patient timeline:
Patient → Appointment → Treatment / Procedure → Invoice

🩺 Patient Records

Full demographic and clinical profile — blood group, marital status, government ID, insurance and family relations — kept in lockstep with a linked Contact so billing and clinical data never drift apart.

📅 Appointments

Physician- and department-scoped scheduling with a draft → confirm → done workflow, automatic exam/consumable tracking, and a direct line into the resulting invoice.

💊 Prescriptions

Multi-line prescriptions capturing medicament, dosage, frequency and duration per drug, feeding straight into pharmacy fulfillment and the patient's clinical history.

🧉 Treatments & Procedures

Billable clinical procedures with kit-product consumption — a suture kit auto-consumes its component parts — plus recurring-procedure scheduling for ongoing care plans.

⚠️ Medical Alerts

Persistent allergy and condition flags on the patient record that surface automatically on appointments, treatments and prescriptions to cut clinical risk.

📊 Evaluations & Vitals

Weight, BMI and vital-sign capture on every visit, charted over time on the patient record so trends are visible at a glance.

🩺‍⚕️ Physician Directory

Specialization, department and schedule per physician — the resource every appointment, treatment and procedure is booked against.

🧾 Integrated Invoicing

Appointments, treatments and procedures generate real Odoo invoices directly, with an exemption flag for care that shouldn't be billed.

📈 Digest & Reporting

Daily/weekly KPI digest mails summarizing patient volume, revenue and clinic activity, straight in management's inbox.
