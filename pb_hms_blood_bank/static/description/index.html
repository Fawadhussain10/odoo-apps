Runs the hospital blood bank on Odoo's own lot/serial and expiry tracking — donor intake and patient issuance both move through the same blood-bag stock lots, so nothing is tracked in a spreadsheet on the side.
Donation / Request → Lot-Tracked Bag → Issue

🫼 Lot-Tracked Blood Bags

Every blood bag is a `stock.lot`-tracked unit with its own blood group, so `product_expiry` governs shelf life and a bag can be traced from donation through to the patient it was issued to.

🥯 Donor Intake

Donations record the donor (Contact or Patient), an "Is Outside" flag for external donors versus in-house collection, location, and the responsible staff member logging the unit into stock.

🧡 Issuance & Billing

Issuing a bag to a patient links the same lot-tracked product through a dedicated blood-bag stock move, with a service product and physician on record for billing the transfusion.
