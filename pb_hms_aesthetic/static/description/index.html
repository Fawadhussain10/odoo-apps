A dedicated aesthetic-medicine workflow on top of PackBytes HMS — structured patient wish assessments, facial mapping evolution tracking, and pre/post questionnaires for cosmetic treatment planning.

📋 Medical & Aesthetic Questionnaires

Configurable questionnaire templates split into medical and aesthetic question types, answered per appointment so intake history is captured consistently before any treatment begins.

👤 Facial Mapping & Evolution

Up to 14 tracked facial parameters per session with free-text evolution notes and a captured patient signature, building a visual progress record across visits.

⚖️ Body Evolution Tracking

Session-by-session body measurements and notes tied to the patient and physician, so weight/shape-management programs have a real audit trail.

📝 Body Concern Assessment

A structured checklist — cellulitis, stretch marks, adiposity, hair removal, skin flaccidity, pre/post-operative status — each mappable straight onto recommended treatment, nutrition and upkeep products.

✨ Facial Concern Assessment

The same structured approach for the face — scars, pigmentation, expression lines, wrinkles, spots, flaccidity — each linked to its own treatment/nutrition/upkeep product recommendations.

📁 Wish Documents & Draft Workflow

Every patient wish record moves through a draft state with attached documents, so the treatment plan is reviewed and approved before it's acted on.
