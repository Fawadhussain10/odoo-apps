Tracks office and premises upkeep as both one-off activities and recurring scheduled tasks — think cleaning registers and maintenance rounds — each with an assignee, a reviewer, and a due date.

✅ One-Off Activities

Log a single task with an assignee, and a separate reviewer who signs it off once it's actually done.

🔄 Recurring Tasks

Define a "repeat every N days/weeks" recurring task that keeps rolling its next-due date forward automatically after each completion.

👀 Assign / Review Split

Every task separates who does the work from who verifies it — a real two-person check baked into the model, not just a comment.

📝 Remarks & Audit Trail

Both activities and recurring tasks carry state and free-text remarks, so completed vs. pending work stays fully documented.
