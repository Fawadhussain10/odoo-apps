Extends pb_webcam's capture-from-camera flow onto the two records where a photo actually matters clinically: the patient, and the physician.

🩺 Patient Photo Capture

Adds the webcam capture button to the Patient form, returning straight back to the Patient list/form action once the photo is saved.

🩺‍⚕️ Physician Photo Capture

Same capture flow on the Physician form, so staff directories stay visually identifiable without a phone or scanner in the loop.

⚡ Zero Extra Setup

Built entirely on pb_webcam's existing mixin and controller — installing this module is the only step needed to light up the button on both forms.
