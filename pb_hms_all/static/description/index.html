One click installs the entire PackBytes HMS suite — clinical care, hospitalization, pharmacy, laboratory & radiology, insurance, patient portal and communications — as a single coherent hospital platform.

🩺 Clinical Core

Patients, appointments, prescriptions, treatments, procedures and physician records — the base every other module builds on.

🏥 Hospitalization & Surgery

In-patient admission, ward/bed management, surgery, emergency care and operation-theater booking, end to end.

💊 Pharmacy & Point of Sale

Batch-wise pricing, expiry tracking, and a full Prescription-Order-to-POS settlement flow at the pharmacy counter.

🧪 Laboratory & Radiology

Request → test → result → invoice flows for both diagnostic domains, including hospitalization-linked variants.

🛡️ Insurance

Claim tracking across hospitalization, laboratory, radiology and surgery, each grounded in its own domain's real workflow.

🌐 Patient Portal & Online Booking

Self-service appointment booking, portal access to records, and next-patient waiting-room screens for the front desk.

📑 Documents, Barcode & Certification

Document management, patient/appointment barcoding, and certificate issuance, all bundled in from day one.

📈 Dashboard & Commission

Role-based KPI dashboards for doctors, receptionists and admins, plus referring/visiting-doctor commission on the revenue they generate.

🏥 Facility Management

Office and premises upkeep tracked as one-off and recurring tasks, right alongside the clinical operations they support.
