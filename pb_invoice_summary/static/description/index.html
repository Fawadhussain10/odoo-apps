Pulls every invoice and payment for a customer or vendor within a date range into one locked, printable statement — grouped by product category, with running totals and payment status:
Pull Invoices & Payments → Group by Category → Lock as Done → Print

📋 Automatic Data Pull

One click gathers every draft/posted invoice and every sent/posted/reconciled payment for the chosen partner inside the selected date range, tagging each one against this summary.

📂 Category-Grouped Lines

Invoice lines are grouped into category sections with a subtotal per category, and refund lines are automatically sign-flipped so the statement nets out correctly.

💰 Untaxed / Tax / Total / Due

Live computed untaxed amount, tax, grand total, total payments received and amount still due — all on the header, recalculated the moment data is refreshed.

🔒 Locked Once Done

Marking a summary Done snapshots the data and blocks further edits or deletion, so a statement that's already been handed to a customer can't quietly change underneath them.
