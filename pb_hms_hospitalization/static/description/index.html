The in-patient (IPD) backbone of PackBytes HMS — admission, ward/bed assignment, nursing and discharge care plans, and the checklists that keep every stay auditable, all rolled up into one hospitalization record.
Admission → Ward / Bed → Care & Checklists → Discharge

🏧 Admission & Ward/Bed Management

Every stay tracks admission type (routine, elective, urgent, emergency), assigned ward and bed — both locked read-only once the stay is cancelled or done — plus legal-case and medico-legal flags with police-report fields when applicable.

✅ Admission & Pre-Ward Checklists

Configurable checklist templates drive a Y/N admission checklist and a separate pre-ward checklist per stay, each with a computed completion percentage so nursing staff can see exactly what's left before transfer.

📋 Care Plans

Structured Nursing Plan and Discharge Plan fields capture the clinical care narrative, locked once the stay reaches cancel/done so the record of care given can't be edited after the fact.

🏪 Accommodation History

A full ward/room accommodation history is kept per stay, so every bed transfer during a hospitalization is timestamped and auditable rather than overwritten.

💉 Discharge Documentation

Diagnosis, clinical summary, examination, investigation, discharge diagnosis, operative note, post-operative course and discharge advice are all captured as structured discharge fields, not free-form notes bolted on afterward.

🧭 Consumables & Surgery Link

Consumable line items track material use during the stay, and hospitalizations link directly into Surgery for patients whose admission includes an operation.
