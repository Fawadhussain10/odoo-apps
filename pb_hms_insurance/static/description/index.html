The core insurance engine for PackBytes HMS — per-patient policies against an insurance company/plan, itemized claims with a document checklist and TPA tracking, and batch claim sheets for settling a period's invoices with an insurer.
Policy → Claim → Documents & Checklist → Approval → Invoice/Payment

💳 Insurance Plans & Policies

Plans configure co-payment amount, insured percentage and a per-service limit independently for appointments and pharmacy, with a per-patient policy record carrying its own policy number against an Insurance Company.

📋 Claims with Document Checklist

Each claim (Draft → Confirmed → Sent for Approval → Approved → Received → Done/Cancelled) tracks requested/passed/received amounts with a computed variance, a TPA (third-party administrator), required-document attachments, and a checklist with a computed completion percentage.

🤝 Claim Origins

A claim's "Claim For" type starts at Appointment/Pharmacy/Other and is extended by the insurance sub-modules to also cover Hospitalization, Surgery, Laboratory and Radiology — one claim model, many clinical origins.

💰 Claim Sheets

Batches a date range of claim invoices per insurance company into a single Claim Sheet with a computed total, for settling a period's receivables with one insurer in one go.
