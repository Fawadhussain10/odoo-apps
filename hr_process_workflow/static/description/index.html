How it works

Every HR change follows the right approval path

Steps come from an editable approval matrix. Each approver gets an activity with a deadline, and late steps are flagged automatically.

+

New Hire

Hiring Manager → HR Operations checks (documents, position, contract, eligibility) → HR Director. One click then creates the employee, contract, bank account and statutory records.

⇄

Salary / Position Change

HR Director → Finance (budget) → Group Finance → GM / CEO. On the effective date a new contract version is created and the full history is kept.

↪

Resignation

Line Manager → HR Director → HR / Finance settlement → Authorized GM. Final settlement with last salary, leave encashment, gratuity and notice shortfall.

✗

Termination

HR Director → Legal review → settlement → CEO / GM. The legal opinion is required before it can move on, and is printed on the form.

Features

Built for real HR teams

⚙ Approval matrix

Steps per request type with approvers by group, user, line manager or department head, plus SLA days.

⏰ Deadlines & activities

Each approver gets a to-do with a due date. Late approvals are highlighted everywhere.

✗ Reject with a reason

Rejections need a comment. Reset to draft and resubmit — earlier rounds stay in the history.

$ Final settlement

Gratuity rules, month days and notice shortfall from company settings; add your own lines.

📅 Applied on the effective date

A daily job applies approved changes and exits when their date arrives — or apply by hand.

🔒 Secure by role

☰ Salary components

Basic, housing, transport or your own — mapped to contract fields or kept as component lines.

Business unit, grade, cost center, employee ID and Arabic name on the employee.

📄 Signed PDF form

A designed PDF per request with changes, salary, settlement, approval trail and signature boxes.
