Lets patients book appointments straight from the website, matched against real physician weekly schedules, with payment collected online at booking time:
Schedule Slot → Online Booking → Payment → Confirmed Appointment

📅 Physician Weekly Schedules

Day-of-week, morning/afternoon periods, and hour-from/hour-to ranges per physician, scoped by department, decide exactly which slots are bookable.

🌐 Self-Service Booking Flow

A guided website flow — pick a slot, confirm appointment details, enter personal data, save — turns an open slot into a confirmed appointment without staff involvement.

💳 Online Payment at Booking

Integrates Odoo's website_payment/account_payment so a booking fee can be shown and collected before the slot is locked in.

🧙 Appointment Scheduler Wizard

A staff-side wizard for building and adjusting physician schedules and slots, instead of hand-editing schedule-line records.

⚙️ Configurable Fee Display

A per-schedule "Show Fees" toggle controls whether the booking price is even surfaced to the patient during checkout.

🔐 Public + Portal Access

Booking works for anonymous website visitors, with the final save step authenticated against the patient's own portal user.
