Bedside nursing operations for PackBytes HMS — timestamped ward rounds that pull live vitals off the patient's evaluation, plus a structured nursing-care checklist for every hospitalized patient.

🩺 Ward Rounds With Live Vitals

Each round pulls weight, height, temperature, heart rate, respiratory rate, systolic/diastolic BP, SpO2, pain level and BMI directly from the linked patient evaluation, so nurses aren't re-entering numbers.

🛏️ Nursing Care Checklist

Position, potty, proximity, pump and personal-needs checks per round, plus urinary catheter status, diuresis volume and blood glucose (glycemia) tracking.

💬 Depression Screening & Evolution

A depression flag and an evolution outcome selection per round, so a patient's day-to-day clinical trajectory during hospitalization is on record, not just their vitals.

👩‍⚕️ Tied To The Hospitalization

Every round links back to the hospitalization and the assigned nurse (from HR employees), keeping a full accountable timeline of who rounded on the patient and when.
