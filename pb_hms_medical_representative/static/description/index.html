Pharma-rep detailing management for PackBytes HMS — track every representative's visits to physicians, what was discussed, and which drug samples were handed out, with full lot traceability.

🧑‍💼 Representative Directory

Medical representatives registered against a Partner record, so their visit history and sample drops tie back into standard Odoo contact data.

📅 Physician Visit Log

Each visit records its purpose, the physician seen, a doctor's remark and a product-description note, moving through a state workflow so visits can be tracked to completion.

💊 Sample Distribution With Lot Tracking

Drug samples handed out per visit are logged as consumable lines with product, UoM, quantity, lot/serial number and unit price, posting a real stock move for full traceability.
