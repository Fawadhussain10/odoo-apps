Extends Certificate Management with patient, physician and appointment context, plus QR-verified, portal-accessible certificates — the module behind medical certificates, fitness letters and sick notes.

🩺 Patient &amp; Physician Linked

Every certificate can carry the patient it was issued for and the physician who issued it, auto-filling the recipient contact and issuing user from those two fields.

📅 Issued Straight from an Appointment

A certificate can be raised directly from an appointment, pre-filled with that visit's patient and physician — no re-entry.

📱 QR-Verified &amp; Portal-Accessible

Every certificate gets a unique verification code and a dedicated portal URL, so a printed certificate can be checked online or the patient can view it in their portal.

📊 Stat Buttons on Patient &amp; Appointment

Both the patient and appointment forms show a live certificate count that opens straight into that record's certificate history.
