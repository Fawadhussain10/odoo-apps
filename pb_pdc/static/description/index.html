Features

Everything your finance team needs

Its own app

A dedicated PDC Cheques app on the home screen, with Customer, Vendor and All Cheques menus.

Automatic journal entries

Registering, depositing, bouncing and clearing each post the right PDC entries and reconcile the invoice.

Due calendar and reminders

See upcoming cheques on a calendar. Scheduled emails remind customers, vendors and your team before the due date.

Kanban board

Cheque cards grouped by status, with amount progress bars, due-date countdowns and activities.

Smart buttons

Jump from a cheque to its invoices, journal entries, journal items and attachments. Partners and invoices link back to their cheques.

Professional reports

A cheque voucher with the amount in words and signature lines, plus a cheque register with summary totals.

Multiple invoices, partial allocation

One cheque can settle several invoices or bills, with a custom amount allocated to each.

Bulk actions

Register, deposit, clear, bounce or cancel many cheques at once from the list view.

Analysis

Pivot and graph views by status, type, partner and due month, with overdue and due-in-7-days filters.

Journal items

Deposit moves the amount from the customer receivable to the PDC Receivable account; clearing moves it from PDC Receivable to the bank.

Choose the PDC receivable account for customer cheques and the PDC payable account for vendor cheques, and set up due-date reminders.

How it works

Up and running in 3 steps

01
Configure PDC accounts

In Settings, choose the PDC receivable and payable accounts, and optionally turn on due-date reminders.

02
Register a cheque

Click Register PDC Cheque on a posted invoice or bill, or create a cheque directly in the PDC Cheques app.

03
Deposit and clear

Move the cheque through deposit and clearing. The entries and the invoice reconciliation happen automatically.
